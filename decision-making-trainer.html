The one rule

Caution is never the wrong answer. The only real mistake is choosing less caution than the day called for. A no-go on a marginal morning is never wrong. The trap is talking yourself into a go.

Built by an airline pilot. This is how professional crews actually think: not memorizing answers, but managing risk through frameworks like PAVE, Pilot, Aircraft, enVironment, External pressure. That habit is the difference between passing a checkride and staying alive after it.
