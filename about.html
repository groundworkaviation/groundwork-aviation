03 / Why Groundwork exists

I remember being
on the other side.

I’ve been both the student and the instructor. As a student, oral exams were the part I found hardest.

I know what I wish I’d had: somewhere to practice saying an answer out loud, get a follow-up question, and work through the gaps before sitting down with an examiner.

That’s the reason I created The Oral Exam Program. It uses an AI examiner to let you practice a spoken conversation. The technology makes that practice available; my experience as a student, instructor, and working pilot is what shaped the idea.

Learning to fly isn’t easy. If I can make part of that process clearer and give you a useful way to practice, that’s what I want Groundwork to do.

Chris Founder, Groundwork Aviation
