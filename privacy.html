Privacy

What we collect, and what we don't.

LAST UPDATED 16 AUGUST 2026

Groundwork Aviation is a small independent publisher of ground-school material for student pilots. This page describes exactly what happens to your data on this website. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. We collect your email address only if you type it into a form. We never see your card details. We use analytics that sets no cookies and cannot follow you to other websites. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever. You can be removed from everything by sending one email.

Who is responsible

Groundwork Aviation, operated from Australia, publishing for a mainly United States readership. For anything on this page, including requests to see or delete your data, write to hello@groundworkaviation.com.

What we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Your email address, and a first name if you give one Only when you submit a form on this site To send you the guide you asked for, and occasional emails about new material
Anonymous page statistics Every page view To see which pages are read. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no profile of you
Order details, if you buy something At checkout, and handled by Gumroad, not by us To deliver the product and handle refunds

That is the entire list. There is no account to create, no profile, no advertising pixel, and no data broker involved.

Payments: we never see your card

All purchases are processed by Gumroad, which is the seller of record for our products. Your card details go to Gumroad and their payment processors, never to us and never through this website. We see the fact of a sale, the product, and the email address used to buy it, so that we can deliver the file and answer support questions. Gumroad's own privacy policy governs what they hold.

Email

Our list is run on MailerLite. If you submit a form here, your address is stored on their platform and used to send you the material you requested plus occasional related emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is immediate and permanent unless you sign up again. We do not share, rent or sell the list.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is worth being specific about this one, because most sites are not: it sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and cannot follow you to any other website. It tells us that a page was viewed, roughly where in the world from, and how quickly it loaded. It does not tell us who you are.

Third parties this page talks to

Loading this website causes your browser to contact a small number of other companies. This is normal for any website, and worth stating plainly:

  • Cloudflare serves the site and provides the analytics described above.
  • MailerLite provides the sign-up forms. Their script may set its own cookies when a form is displayed.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request. We are moving these fonts onto our own server to remove this.
  • Gumroad, only if you click through to buy something.

How long we keep things

Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe or ask to be deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require. Analytics data is aggregate and is retained by Cloudflare on their own schedule; it is not tied to you.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you. Email hello@groundworkaviation.com and we will action it. We will not ask you why.

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, the GDPR gives you those rights formally, and our lawful basis is your consent for email and our legitimate interest in understanding aggregate readership for analytics. If you are in California, the CCPA gives you similar rights. We do not sell personal information as either law defines it, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

This site is intended for people training to become pilots and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us an email address, tell us and we will remove it.

Security, honestly stated

The site is served over HTTPS and holds no database of its own. Your email lives with MailerLite and your purchase data with Gumroad, both of which are established providers with their own security programs. No system is perfectly secure, and we would rather say that than claim otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top. If the change affects how we use your email, we will tell you by email rather than quietly editing this page.
