The gap nobody fixes

You can pass the written and still freeze at the oral.

Most courses drill you to recognise the right answer out of A, B and C. But the examiner doesn't give you three options — they ask you to explain it. That's where shallow prep falls apart. We teach the why, so it holds under pressure.
