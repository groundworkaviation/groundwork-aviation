A few simple steps

Find your purchase.
Share your experience.

  1. 01

    Sign in to Gumroad

    Use the account associated with your purchase, then find your Groundwork product on the Reviews page.

  2. 02

    Add your review

    Choose a star rating and write a few specific sentences about your experience. Then select Post review to submit your written feedback.

Bought without a Gumroad account?

Open your original Gumroad receipt email and select View content. Enter the email address you used at checkout if asked, then leave your review from the product’s content page.
