THE IDEA TO TAKE AWAY

Weight, balance, and performance are separate checks. Passing one does not establish that the others are acceptable.

Density altitude describes air density

Density altitude is pressure altitude corrected for nonstandard temperature. Higher temperature or lower pressure reduces air density; added humidity also reduces density. High density altitude generally reduces takeoff and climb performance. Use the aircraft’s POH/AFM charts and their stated conditions for actual numbers. FAA PHAK, Chapter 11

Field elevation and density altitude are different quantities. The same runway can have substantially different performance conditions on different days. Start the performance calculation with the inputs the particular chart requests, such as pressure altitude, temperature, weight, and wind. FAA PHAK, Chapter 11

How much weight, and where is it?

Weight is the total load. Balance is the location of the center of gravity (CG). An airplane can be below its maximum weight and still have a CG outside the approved range. Neither a forward nor an aft limit is optional. FAA PHAK, Chapter 10

A CG too far forward can leave inadequate elevator authority for landing. A CG too far aft can reduce longitudinal stability and the ability to recover from stalls and spins. Use the actual loading envelope and current weight-and-balance information for the aircraft. FAA PHAK, Chapter 10

Move the load and watch the moment change

Moment equals weight × arm. Arm is the distance from the reference datum. Total moment divided by total weight gives the CG location. This fictional arithmetic example shows the relationship; it has no approved aircraft limits. FAA PHAK, Chapter 10

Keep the checks separate

Check your understandingYou are under maximum weight. Have you proved the airplane is correctly loaded?

No. You must also check the CG against the approved envelope. Being under a weight limit does not establish acceptable balance.

FAA PHAK, Chapter 10
Check your understandingIn this model, what changes when you slide the same 200 lb load aft?

The load’s arm and moment increase. Total weight stays the same and the combined CG moves aft. Real-aircraft acceptability depends on its approved envelope.
